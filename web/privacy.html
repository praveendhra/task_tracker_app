Privacy policy

Task Tracker: Step by Step

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Task Tracker (“the app”) is made and run by an individual developer. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and how you can delete it. The same policy covers the website at task.praveendhra.com and the Android and iOS apps.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google or Apple. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.
  • Your content: the tasks, steps, notes, due dates, priorities and tags you create.
  • Settings: theme, whether the daily email is on, the hour to send it, and your time zone.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising IDs. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking.

How it is used

  • To sync your tasks between your devices.
  • To send the optional daily summary email, only if you turn it on in Settings.

Your data is never sold, shared for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) and encrypted in transit and at rest. Daily emails are delivered through Resend. These providers process data only to run the service.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and everything in it at any time from Settings → Delete account in the app. Deletion is immediate and permanent. If you can't open the app, see how to request deletion.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change and significant changes will be noted in the app.
