Task Tracker: Step by Step

You can delete your Task Tracker account and all of its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Task Tracker and sign in.
  2. Open the account menu and choose Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. You may be asked to sign in again.

This works on Android, iPhone and at task.praveendhra.com.

By email

If you can't sign in, email tasks@mail.praveendhra.com from the address on your account with the subject “Delete my account”. It will be deleted within 30 days and you'll get a confirmation.

What is deleted

Your sign-in, every task, step, note and tag, your settings and the daily email. Nothing is kept afterwards, and deletion can't be undone.
